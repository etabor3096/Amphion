How to use

Press Play. The orange line is the playhead. Click empty space to jump there. Click a note or a chord to select it (and jump to it), then fix it below. Space plays and pauses. Corrections are kept until you download them; reloading the page loses them.
Several notes at once: drag a box around them, or Shift/⌘-click to add or remove one (Shift-drag adds a box). Zoom out to box a longer stretch. ↑/↓ move them a semitone, Delete deletes them, Esc clears the selection.
