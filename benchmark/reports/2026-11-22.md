## Reading this report

This is the first baseline: the pipeline on `dev` at 3459052 (Demucs `htdemucs_6s`, Basic Pitch for guitar with onset snapping, pYIN for bass, madmom beats and chords, tuned tab weights), run on the 3 proof-of-concept songs.

**Against the RISK-01 bars** (60% guitar note F-measure on uploads, 70% chords; the bars are not lowered, A9):

| Bar | Result | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guitar notes, uploads | 62.4% (Maple Syrup only) | Above 60%, on one song |
| Chords, uploads | 79.7% (Maple Syrup only) | Above 70%, on one song |
| Chords, all songs | 59.7% | Below 70%; the two phone recordings pull it down (46.1%) |
| Guitar notes, mic recordings | 20.4% (Know Your Name only) | Far below 60% |
| Bass notes, mic recordings | 16.1% | Far below 60% |

**Caveats**

- **In-sample.** Bass notes, bass tab weights, chord input and onset snapping were all tuned on these same 3 songs, so every number here is optimistic. The remaining RC-10 songs have not been used for tuning and should be scored once when they are added.
- **One song per cell.** Guitar and upload results come from a single song, so they are provisional (OQ-07).
- **Phone recordings are the weak spot.** The bass fundamental is 5 to 10 times weaker than its harmonics on the mic recordings, and the guitar part competes with the room sound.
- **Guitar string and fret on Maple Syrup (33.6%)** is mostly barre shape against open shape for the same pitches. The pipeline picks the open shape, which is the beginner-friendly choice (DD-15), and the reference uses the published tab's shape.
- **Tried and rejected.** Tuning correction, ensembling, dropping short chords, gap filling and Chordino did not help. SwiftF0 and torchcrepe did not beat pYIN for bass. `htdemucs_ft` helped one song (With Rob as My Witness, 0.164 to 0.490) and left the other two unchanged, at about twice the run time, so it was not adopted.
- **Run time** is about 130 to 150 s per 3-minute song on this Mac (CPU), so it does not meet RC-08's 2-minute goal yet.
